      Before you go

      Visiting a family farm

      Book stays ahead

      Farm stays are small and fill up — book before you travel, especially in winter.

      Ask before entering

      Most farms are family homes and workplaces. Call ahead even for a short visit.

      Buy at the gate

      Fruit, honey, spices and pickles bought direct support the farmer most.

      Respect the animals

      Keep a calm distance from dairy cows and hives, and follow the farmer's lead.
