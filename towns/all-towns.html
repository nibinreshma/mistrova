18 Towns · Wayanad, Kerala

Every town in Wayanad

one plateau, eighteen places to start from

Wayanad is not one place you visit but a plateau of small towns, each with its own reason to stop — a market, a temple, a dam, a research station, a road into the forest. This page holds all 18, grouped by the part of the district they sit in, so you can pick the one your trip should actually be built around.

The three main towns

Where the district does its business

Kalpetta, Bathery and Mananthavady are the taluk towns. Between them they hold the buses, the banks, the hospitals and the markets — if a journey across Wayanad changes direction, it changes direction in one of these three.

780 m

District headquarters · NH 766

The administrative centre of Wayanad, strung along the national highway between the ghat and the plateau.

The ghat and the rain belt

The western edge, under the cloud

The first towns you meet climbing the Thamarassery ghat, and the wettest ground in the district. Rainforest, tea, cardamom and the dam sit within half an hour of each other here.

The southern plateau

Open country, and the oldest marks

Flatter, drier and more farmed than the west. This is the belt that holds Edakkal, the research station, the Karapuzha basin and most of the district’s nurseries.

How to use these pages

  • Pick a baseMost visitors stay in one town and drive out from it. The west is wet and green, the south is open and farmed, the north and east are closest to forest.
  • DistancesNothing in Wayanad is far, but the roads are slow. Each town page opens with how long it takes to reach the three main towns and Kozhikode.
  • EssentialsEvery town page lists its hospital, police station, village office and panchayat, with phone numbers where they are published.
